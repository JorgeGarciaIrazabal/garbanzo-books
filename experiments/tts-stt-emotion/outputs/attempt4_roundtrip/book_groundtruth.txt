Once upon a time, in a tiny cottage at the edge of a mossy forest, there lived a small red panda named Pip. Every morning, Pip put on a yellow raincoat and set out to find a new friend. One day, Pip found a blue feather on the mossy path! It was longer than Pip's paw, and it shimmered in the morning light. Pip wondered, who does this feather belong to? Deep in the forest, an old owl watched from a hollow tree. Hoo, said the owl. I have been waiting for you, little one. Pip and the owl became friends at once! They shared acorn-cakes, and told stories of the wind. But soon, the sun began to set, and Pip felt a little afraid. Do not worry, said the owl. I will fly you home. They flew above the trees, and the world looked very small. Good night, little panda, said the owl, until tomorrow. And Pip fell asleep, smiling, with the blue feather tucked under one ear.
