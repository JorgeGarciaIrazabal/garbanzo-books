# The Strongest Wizard — Art Style Guide

## Visual Identity
**Dreamy pastel storybook** — soft as a bedtime whisper, bright as a child's determination. Every illustration feels like a memory of golden hour: blush pinks, mint greens, lavenders, and warm cream backgrounds with floating motes of magic light.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Background | Cream | `#fdf6e3` | Page base, sky, walls |
| Primary | Blush Pink | `#f4c2c2` | Aria's cloak, warm accents, spell glow |
| Secondary | Mint Green | `#a8e6cf` | Meadow grass, healing magic, friend accents |
| Accent | Lavender | `#c7b8ea` | Mystery magic, twilight scenes, protective wards |
| Highlight | Golden Hour | `#ffd93d` | Spell sparkles, candlelight, triumph moments |
| Shadow | Dusk Lavender | `#6b5b95` | Deep shadows, night scenes, rival wizard auras |

## Line & Texture
- **Whisper-thin outlines** — barely-there, rounded, hand-drawn with colored pencil
- **No harsh edges** — shapes blur into each other like watercolor bleeding
- **Paper texture visible** — subtle grain throughout, feels like a real picture book
- **Magic effects** — floating particles, ripple distortions, soft glows (never harsh flares)

## Lighting
- **Golden-hour default** — warm, directional, long soft shadows
- **Rim light on characters** — subtle halo separating them from backgrounds
- **Magic as light source** — spells emit gentle glow, not harsh beams
- **Floating motes** — tiny sparkles in air where magic lingers

## Composition
- **Eye-level, character-centered** — we see the world from Aria's height
- **Slight storybook tilt** — pages feel like opened book spreads
- **Text zone** — lower third reserved, soft cream panel with lavender edge glow at 85% opacity
- **Breathing room** — negative space around characters for text legibility

## Character Design Principles
- **Round, soft shapes** — no sharp angles on heroes
- **Expressive eyes** — large, reflective, show every emotion
- **Magic manifests visually** — Aria's hair floats slightly when casting, cloak ripples with spell energy
- **Friends distinct but harmonious** — each has a signature color from palette

## Environment Moods
| Location | Mood | Key Visuals |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Whispering Village | Cozy | Warm lights in windows, smoke curling, ward stones pulsing |
| Great Oak Library | Wondrous | Floating books, golden light shafts, endless shelves |
| Training Meadow | Playful | Charred/frozen/flowered dummies, kids laughing, messy magic |
| The Wilds | Mysterious | Misted forests, glowing crystals, silhouette towers |

## Do / Don't

**DO:**
- Keep it soft, warm, inviting
- Show effort and practice (messy spells, determined faces)
- Let magic feel wondrous, not scary
- Friends touching, laughing, helping each other

**DON'T:**
- Harsh contrasts or saturated neons
- Scary monster designs (make them weird/cute)
- Perfect, effortless magic — show the work
- Adult proportions — keep childlike scale
- Thick outlines or comic-book styling

## Reference Touchstones
- Studio Ghibli pastel backgrounds (Kiki's Delivery Service, My Neighbor Totoro)
- Rebecca Sugar's color styling (Steven Universe backgrounds)
- Classic 90s picture books (The Jolly Postman, Brambly Hedge)
- Watercolor + colored pencil hybrid technique
