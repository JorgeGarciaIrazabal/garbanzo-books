# Lunaria Crystal — Style Guide

> The locked visual identity for this world. Every illustration is assembled from the
> `art_style` block in `world.yaml`; this doc is the human-readable art direction.

## Palette
| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| midnight-cobalt | `#1a2a6c` | sky |
| moonlight-gold | `#fde08a` | primary |
| sailor-rose | `#ff8fb1` | accent |
| cloud-cream | `#fff5e1` | background |
| starlight-silver | `#d6e3ff` | highlight |
| crystal-violet | `#9a7bd1` | shadow |
| luna-black | `#1a1a2e` | detail |

## Look & feel
- **Medium:** bright magical-girl anime illustration with cel shading and sparkles
- **Line:** clean, confident, glossy
- **Lighting:** glowing, sparkly, big shining eye highlights, rim light from the upside-down moon
- **Mood:** magical, warm, dreamy, hopeful

## The upside-down moon
- It is the **defining** visual element — always present, always smiling, always glowing.
- The crescent points DOWN (like a smile, not a frown).
- It should be the brightest thing in the sky and sit high above the crystal tower.
- When the kingdom is sad it dims to a soft lavender; when happy it glows gold.

## Do
- Always show the upside-down moon in sky shots.
- Keep eyes BIG and SHINY with star highlights (Sailor Moon signature).
- Use lots of sparkles, lens-flares, and glittery star particles.
- Keep characters on-model (use the appearance tokens + reference sheets).
- Reserve the lower third of each page as calm negative space for text.

## Don't
- No photorealism, 3D, or muted colors.
- No scary or distorted faces.
- No extra fingers, gore, or dark horror.
- No text baked into the art (text is overlaid by the reader).
- Don't break the world's `rules` (see world.yaml).
- Don't make the moon face up — it's always upside down (a smile).
