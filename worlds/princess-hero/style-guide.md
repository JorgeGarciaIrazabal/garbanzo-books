# Princess Hero — Art Style Guide

## Quick Reference
- **Medium:** Soft watercolor with colored-pencil texture
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 (full-bleed pages)
- **Text Zone:** Lower third, soft cream rounded panel at 85% opacity with subtle watercolor bleed edges
- **Font:** Andika (dyslexia-friendly)

## Visual Identity by Time of Day

### DAY SCENES (Princess Liora)
- **Lighting:** Warm golden-hour glow, soft and diffuse
- **Mood:** Bright, bustling, slightly absurd formality
- **Composition:** Eye-level, centered on Liora; lots of background detail (courtiers, cakes, guards)
- **Colors:** Dawn-cream base, sunlit-peach and royal-lavender dominance, sage-mint greenery
- **Key Details:** Liora in princess gowns (lavender/peach), star earring visible, tiara slightly askew, the cat nearby looking unimpressed

### NIGHT SCENES (Star-Strider)
- **Lighting:** Cool moonlight with warm lantern pools; stars as active sparkles
- **Mood:** Magical, kinetic, secret, playful
- **Composition:** Dynamic low angles for hero moments; rooftop silhouettes; dramatic diagonals
- **Colors:** Midnight-blue base, hero-teal and star-gold as glowing accents, rose-blush for warmth
- **Key Details:** Star-Strider in DIY gear (bedsheet cape, pillowcase mask with star charm, sock-gloves), star charm catches light, cat sidekick on shoulder or leading the way

## Character Visual Anchors (For Consistency)

### Princess Liora / Star-Strider
- **Face:** Round, expressive, large eyes (hero-teal), rose-blush cheeks
- **Hair:** Dark, slightly messy curls (escaping tiara by day; flying free by night)
- **Star Link:** Single star earring (day) ↔ star charm on mask (night) — SAME gold
- **Day Outfit:** Lavender/peach princess dress, practical hidden pockets
- **Night Outfit:** Bedsheet cape (hero-teal lined), pillowcase mask, sock-gloves, regular clothes underneath

### The Cat (Sidekick)
- **Appearance:** Sleek, midnight-blue fur, one white star-patch on chest, hero-teal eyes
- **Expression:** Permanently unimpressed, half-lidded judgment
- **Role:** Only one who knows the secret; silent commentator

## Composition Guidelines

### Page Layout
- **Full-bleed illustration** — art goes to all edges
- **Text zone:** Lower third reserved; keep key action/faces out of this band
- **Scrim:** Soft cream rounded panel, 85% opacity, subtle watercolor bleed edges
- **Safe area:** Center 80% for critical character moments

### Dynamic Beats (Action Pages)
- Low angle looking up at Star-Strider on rooftop
- Diagonal rooftop lines creating momentum
- Motion lines as colored-pencil scribbles (hero-teal/star-gold)
- Lanterns/star-trails as motion streaks

### Quiet Beats (Day Court / Night Planning)
- Eye-level, centered symmetry gently broken
- Background characters doing funny micro-actions
- Cat in foreground as framing device

---

## Texture & Technique Notes

- **Watercolor:** Wet-on-wet for skies/backgrounds; granulating pigments for stone/foliage
- **Colored Pencil:** Overlaid for texture, outlines, facial details, motion energy
- **Paper Grain:** Visible cold-press texture throughout
- **Bleed Edges:** Intentional color bleeds at scrim edges and illustration borders
- **White Space:** Preserve paper white for highlights (stars, eye-catchlights, cape edges)

---

## Do's and Don'ts

### DO
- Mix day/night palettes in transition scenes (twilight = both)
- Let the cat's stare break the fourth wall
- Show DIY hero gear imperfections (tape, safety pins, uneven eye-holes)
- Make backgrounds tell jokes (guard asleep, pigeon stealing crown)
- Use star-gold for ANY magic/glow — it's the world's "magic color"

### DON'T
- Make night scenes scary-dark (keep lantern pools warm)
- Draw generic spandex superhero suits — DIY only
- Show Liora's face fully in Star-Strider mask (eyes only)
- Use pure black — midnight-blue is the darkest value
- Forget the star earring/charm — it's the visual anchor
- Make the Queen or Guard competent — they're lovingly useless
