# The Baby Luka — Art Style Guide

> **One line:** Bold flat cartoon, anime-cel-shaded, bright Pokémon-Kanto palette. Storybook meets anime key art.

Every illustration in this world must look like it came from the same studio — a mash-up of
**Pokémon anime production cels** and a **bold flat storybook cartoon**. Clean black outlines,
crisp cel-shading, punchy colors, dynamic baby-eye angles. Funny and playful, never soft or
watercolored.

## Medium & technique

- **Bold flat vector cartoon** with **cel-shading** — 2 to 3 flat tones per color, hard shadow
  edges, **no gradient blur**.
- **Thick confident black outlines** — graphic, anime production-cel style. Not sketchy, not
  faint.
- **Bright even anime lighting** with occasional **graphic sunburst / flare motifs** for comedy
  beats. Punchy rim accents on characters.
- **Dynamic anime angles** — default to a **low baby-eye POV** (we're seeing the world from
  Luka's height). Use dramatic close-ups and the occasional fisheye for comic effect.

| 🔵 kanto sky blue | `#3B9BE0` | sky |

| 🟣 soft shadow purple | `#5B4A8A` | shadow |

The palette is bright and saturated — this is a comedy world, not a muted storybook. Sky blue
and cream paper carry backgrounds; sunshine yellow / bulba green are the workhorse character &
prop colors; pokeball red and pikachu orange pop for accents and punchlines; soft shadow purple
is the only cel-shadow tone.

## The prompt style block (injected into EVERY image)

> bold flat vector cartoon, anime-style cel-shading with crisp 2-3 tone flats, thick confident
> black outlines, bright punchy Pokémon-anime palette (#FFD23F sunshine yellow, #EE1515 pokeball
> red, #FFA52B pikachu orange, #3B9BE0 kanto sky blue, #5BCB5F bulba green, #5B4A8A soft shadow
> purple, #FFF4E0 cream paper), graphic sunburst lighting motifs, dynamic baby-eye-low POV,
> clean uncluttered composition with generous negative space, storybook-meets-anime-key-art
> mood, playful and funny, no text, no photorealism

## Negative prompt (always applied)

photorealism, 3D render, soft watercolor, gradient shading, blurry edges, scary or distorted
faces, extra fingers, text artifacts, watermark, cluttered composition, dark mood, realistic
proportions, gore, weapons

## Text on image

- **Placement:** lower-third, full-bleed image above.
- **Scrim:** cream (`#FFF4E0`) rounded panel at 90% opacity with a thick black outline — an
  anime speech-bubble feel that stays legible over busy art.
- **Font:** Andika (dyslexia-friendly).
- The image prompt must keep the lower third clear of important detail so text never overlaps
  a face or a key gag.

## Do / Don't

**DO**
- Draw Pokémon on-model as their Gen 1 (Kanto) anime designs — recognizable, cute, toddler-scale.
- Use anime reaction beats: sweat-drops, action lines, dramatic shine — but storybook-restrained, one per spread max.
- Keep compositions clean and uncluttered — generous negative space, one clear focal gag.
- Shoot from Luka's height (baby-eye low) by default.

**DON'T**
- Don't use soft watercolor, muted palettes, or gradient shading — this world is bold and flat.
- Don't draw scary, dark, or "realistic-proportioned" Pokémon — everything stays cute and comedic.
- Don't clutter the frame with extra Pokémon the scene didn't call for — one gag per spread.
- Don't let Pokémon appear giant or threatening — they're toddler-scale and friendly.
