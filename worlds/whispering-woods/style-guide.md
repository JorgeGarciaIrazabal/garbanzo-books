# The Whispering Woods — Style Guide

> The locked visual identity. Every illustration is assembled from `art_style` in
> `world.yaml`; this is the human-readable art direction.

## Palette
| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| cream | `#f4e1c1` | background |
| forest-sage | `#6b8f71` | primary |
| warm-terracotta | `#d98a5b` | accent |
| acorn-gold | `#e8b84b` | highlight (the magic motif) |
| dusk-blue | `#3d5a73` | shadow / night |
| berry-red | `#c0392b` | accent (Pip's raincoat) |

## Look & feel
- **Medium:** soft watercolor with colored-pencil texture; visible hand-painted paper grain.
- **Line:** gentle, rounded ink lines — nothing sharp or jagged.
- **Light:** warm golden-hour glow, dappled through leaves, gentle rim light on characters.
- **Camera:** low, child's-eye-level; the little folk loom large, the world feels cozy.
- **Mood:** reassuring wonder. Even the Tall Dark Pines are hushed, never threatening.

## Do
- Keep characters perfectly on-model — use each character's `appearance_token` + reference sheet.
- Reserve the **lower third** of every page as calm negative space for the text scrim.
- Let the **acorn-gold** glow appear wherever the woods' gentle magic is present.
- Use the full palette but keep terracotta/berry-red as accents, sage as the dominant green.

## Don't
- No photorealism, harsh shadows, sharp teeth, or scary faces.
- No text baked into the art — the reader overlays text.
- Never break the world `rules`: the woods are always safe in the end; magic stays small.

## Recurring motifs to weave in
A glowing golden acorn · the hum of the trees · stepping stones across the Mossy Stream ·
firefly lanterns.
