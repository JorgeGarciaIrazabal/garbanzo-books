# Super Girls — Style Guide

> The locked visual identity for this world. Every illustration is assembled from the
> `art_style` block in `world.yaml`; this doc is the human-readable art direction.

## Palette
| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| cream | `#f4e1c1` | background |
| sage | `#6b8f71` | primary |
| terracotta | `#d98a5b` | accent |
| dusk-blue | `#3d5a73` | shadow |
| starlight-gold | `#f4d58a` | highlight |
| lavender-mist | `#b8a8d1` | accent |
| rose-quartz | `#e8b4b8` | accent |

## Look & feel
- **Medium:** soft watercolor with colored-pencil texture
- **Line:** gentle, rounded, hand-drawn
- **Lighting:** warm, gentle rim light with subtle starlight sparkle
- **Mood:** cozy, reassuring, wonder-filled, gently magical, **playfully funny**

## Character color cues (for reference — each girl's power palette)
- **Clara (Courage/Shield):** starlight-gold + cream
- **Lizzy (Curiosity/Lantern):** lavender-mist + sage
- **Catalina (Kindness/Vines):** rose-quartz + sage
- **Ziba (Imagination/Illusions):** lavender-mist + starlight-gold
- **Barnaby (Comedy/Chaos):** cream + terracotta (chubby owl-rabbit, oversized star-patch on chest, one ear always flopped)

## Do
- Keep characters on-model (use the appearance tokens + reference sheets).
- Reserve the lower third of each page as calm negative space for text.
- Stay within the palette above; let starlight-gold catch the eye for magical moments.
- Show the star-mark on wrists glowing faintly when adventure calls.
- Pajamas may subtly shift pattern/color to hint at each girl's power.
- **Barnaby:** draw him mid-shenanigan — sitting on the map, tangled in a dream-vine, wearing a crayon mustache, asleep in a teacup. His expressions: baffled, smug, distracted, delighted.
- Night-Mares: fuzzy, rounded, more misunderstood than scary — dark dusk-blue forms with soft edges, dissolving into motes of light when soothed. **They react to Barnaby with visible confusion.**

## Don't
- No photorealism, harsh shadows, or scary faces.
- No text baked into the art (text is overlaid by the reader).
- Don't break the world's `rules` (see world.yaml).
- No neon colors, sharp angular lines, or dark gritty atmosphere.
- Night-Mares should never look truly villainous — they're worries given form.
- Don't show the girls' waking bodies moving during dream missions (they stay asleep).
- **Don't make Barnaby look "cool" or competent — his charm is sincere cluelessness.**

## References & Inspiration
- Beatrice Alemagna — expressive, textural watercolor with emotional depth
- Isabelle Arsenault — gentle line, quiet emotional resonance
- Classic Little Golden Books — warmth, approachability, timeless cozy feel
- Richard Scarry — busy, funny details tucked into cozy scenes
